3. General information on data processing

As a matter of principle, we process our visitors' personal data only to the extent necessary to provide a functioning website and our content. The legal bases are in particular Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests) and § 25 TDDDG (protection of privacy in terminal equipment).

4. Hosting via GitHub Pages (third-country transfer, USA)

This website is delivered via the GitHub Pages service. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA - a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation.

Each time a page is requested, your browser automatically transmits data to GitHub's servers, where it is stored in what are known as server log files. This typically includes:

  • the IP address of your device
  • the date and time of access
  • the URL requested and the referrer
  • the browser type and version used
  • the operating system used

Purpose: technical provision of the website, maintaining stability and security, protection against misuse and attacks.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the reliable, secure and low-cost delivery of this open-source project page.

Third-country transfer: GitHub also processes data in the USA. The USA is a third country within the meaning of the GDPR. The transfer takes place on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Decision 2023/1795); Microsoft Corporation, GitHub's parent company, is certified under the Data Privacy Framework. In addition or in the alternative, standard contractual clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR (Implementing Decision 2021/914/EU) apply. Despite these safeguards, it cannot be entirely ruled out that US security authorities may access data on the basis of US law (in particular FISA 702, CLOUD Act).

Storage period: the retention period for the server logs is determined by GitHub. We ourselves have no direct access to this log data. Further information is available in GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

5. Storage in local browser storage (localStorage)

So that your display preferences persist across page views, the website stores the following values in your browser's localStorage:

Values stored in local browser storage, with content, purpose and retention period
KeyContentPurposeRetention
yuvomi-theme e.g. light / dark retaining the chosen colour scheme until you delete it
yuvomi-lang e.g. de / en retaining the chosen language until you delete it

Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG. Storing these preferences is strictly necessary in order to provide the telemedia service you have expressly requested - namely displaying the website in the language and colour scheme you selected. Consent is therefore not required. These values are not transmitted to third parties or to any server.

How to delete these values: via your browser settings under "Delete site data" / "Cookies and other site data", or via the developer tools (Application → Local Storage → yuvomi.cloud).

Note: earlier versions of this website used the keys oikos-theme and oikos-lang. These are still read until you switch language or colour scheme again.

6. No cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no external requests

This website does not use cookies in the legal sense. In particular, it uses no analytics or tracking services (e.g. Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible), no marketing pixels (e.g. Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn pixels), no social media embeds and no reCAPTCHA.

No fonts are loaded at all: the pages use only the system fonts already present on your device. Google Fonts is not included. No external service is contacted when a page is opened - any figures shown (for example the number of GitHub "stars") are embedded statically into the page before publication, so your browser does not need to establish a connection to any third party for them.

We delete the data as soon as it is no longer required to achieve that purpose and no statutory retention periods (e.g. § 257 HGB, § 147 AO) prevent deletion.

We will then no longer process the personal data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to assert, exercise or defend legal claims.
